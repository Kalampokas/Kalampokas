# George Kalampokas — Smart Home & IoT Systems Integrator

I design and build **local-first, autonomous building automation systems (Local-First Smart Home & Security)** powered by **Home Assistant**, without reliance on third-party cloud servers. I focus on hardware and network reliability, integrating heterogeneous subsystems (lighting, HVAC, security, energy management), and delivering clean, mobile-first user interfaces.

---

## Technical Stack & Architecture

* **Core Platform:** Home Assistant OS (Bare-metal / Mini PC), YAML, Jinja2, Diagnostics & Trace Debugging
* **Local Protocols:** Zigbee (ZHA & Zigbee2MQTT), MQTT, ESPHome, Local APIs / Fieldbus Integration
* **Security & Safety:** Alarmo, Physical Zigbee Keypads, Leak & Smoke Detection, iOS Critical Alerts
* **Custom Hardware & Nodes:** ESP32 / ESP8266, I2C OLED Displays, Environmental & mmWave Presence Sensors
* **Remote Access & Resilience:** Tailscale VPN, Automated Backups, Battery & Power Protection Logic
